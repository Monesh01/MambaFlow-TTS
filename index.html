Welcome to the Demonstration

Hello and welcome to the MambaFlow Text-to-Speech audio demonstration site. This research presents an exhaustive study of 7 distinct flow-matching architectures spanning both 100-mel (BigVGAN 24kHz) and 80-mel (HiFi-GAN 22.05kHz) acoustic pipelines. We empirically prove that multiscale downsampling and transposed convolutions introduce severe temporal aliasing ("comb-filtering"), while our proposed champion—the Tetra Sequential Decoder—operates entirely at native $1\times$ resolution to achieve studio-grade naturalness and the highest objective speech intelligibility (STOI: 0.739, PESQ: 1.084).

Architectural Topologies Overview

Comparing design trade-offs across native $1\times$ resolution, feature pyramids, downsampled cascades, and bidirectional vs causal state-space modeling.

1. Tetra Sequential (100-Mel, BigVGAN 24kHz) 🏆

Native $1\times$ full temporal resolution. Zero downsampling, zero transposed convolutions. Eliminates multiscale aliasing and comb-filtering completely. Achieves champion STOI (0.739) and lowest high-frequency MAE (0.633).

2. BiDirectional-Mamba2 (80-Mel, HiFi-GAN 22.05kHz) 🥈

Dual-channel forward + backward SSM decoder with ConvNeXt spatial mixing. Captures bidirectional phonetic context across the entire utterance, outperforming causal Mamba-2 by 18% lower CFM loss and sharper MCD (32.11 dB).

3. Staircase-XTEncoder (100-Mel, BigVGAN 24kHz) 🥉

Multi-scale feature pyramid ($1\times \to 1/2\times \to 1/4\times$) with cross-attention local smoothing. Trained end-to-end as a robust backbone that served as initialization for Tetra.

4. Staircase-Mamba2 (100-Mel, BigVGAN 24kHz)

ResNet-style feature pyramid using purely Bidirectional Mamba-2 and ConvNeXt blocks with lateral skip connections. Preserves mid-frequencies well.

5. TwoStage-Mamba2 (100-Mel, BigVGAN 24kHz)

Cascaded dual-stage downsampling ($1/2\times$ and $1/4\times$). Extremely fast (62.6x RT), but transposed convolutions introduce audible comb-filtering phase artifacts.

6. UNet-OneBottleneck (100-Mel, BigVGAN 24kHz)

Single bottleneck lightweight U-Net topology. Highly efficient (8.50M params, 66.5x RT speedup), but aggressive bottleneck compression slightly softens formant clarity.

7. UniDirectional-Mamba2 (80-Mel, HiFi-GAN 22.05kHz)

Forward-only causal Mamba-2 decoder. High throughput (43.6x RT), but lacks future co-articulation context, resulting in higher validation loss (0.3917) and lower STOI (0.697).
