## 1. Executive Summary

This report outlines the objective testing metrics of the custom MambaFlow-TTS architecture on the validation set. Testing was executed to validate the Real-Time Factor (RTF) and the objective reconstruction accuracy (MSE / MAE) between the model's generated acoustic prior distributions and the ground-truth LJSpeech mel-spectrograms.

The testing pipeline automatically denormalizes the predicted mels, aligns them with the ground truth, computes pixel-level error metrics, and passes them through the BigVGAN vocoder to ensure they are synthesis-ready. Peak normalization to `0.95` was applied to all synthesized outputs to prevent clipping.

> [!TIP]
> **Performance Highlights**
> - The model achieves a fast global **RTF of 0.267** on GPU, meaning 1 second of audio takes only ~0.26 seconds to synthesize (including both CFM ODE and Vocoder time).
> - The low MAE scores indicate strong adherence to the ground truth acoustic profiles while allowing slight natural variances due to the probabilistic nature of the ODE solver.
